# Privacy

The connector's privacy notice is published at
https://pleso-mcp--product-team-products.europe-west4.hosted.app/privacy

Its source is `privacyPage()` in [`src/pages.ts`](../src/pages.ts). Pleso's main Privacy Policy is at
https://pleso.me/en/legal/privacy-policy.

In short, the connector receives only search filters (language, short concern keywords, optional preferences). It
stores none of them, creates no accounts and sets no cookies. Request arguments are never logged.
